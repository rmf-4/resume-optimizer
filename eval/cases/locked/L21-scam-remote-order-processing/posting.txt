Remote Order Processing Coordinator (Entry Level, Work From Home)
Glimmerquay Fulfillment Partners LLC
United States - 100% Remote

About Us

Glimmerquay Fulfillment Partners LLC is a fast-growing e-commerce fulfillment company serving online sellers nationwide. Due to rapid expansion we are urgently hiring Remote Order Processing Coordinators to start this week. No experience is necessary, we provide full training!

Pay: $38.00 - $45.00 per hour, paid weekly. Earn up to $1,900 per week with flexible hours (as little as 15 hours per week).

What You Will Do

- Receive customer orders by email and enter them into our online order portal
- Confirm order details and shipping addresses with customers
- Receive packages at your home address, remove the original packaging and labels, and reship items to our international customers using the prepaid labels we send you
- Update the daily order tracker in Google Sheets
- Communicate with your team lead through our messaging channel

What We Are Looking For

- Basic computer skills and a reliable internet connection
- Good attention to detail
- Able to work independently from home
- Must be 18 years or older
- Must have a personal checking account for direct deposit

1. Interviews are done by text chat only. Message our Hiring Manager on Telegram at @GQFP_HiringDesk_EXAMPLE and reply "INTERESTED" to start your interview. Do not call the company.

2. Before your interview, send the following so our payroll team can set up your direct deposit in advance: full legal name, date of birth, Social Security number, a photo of your driver's license (front and back), and your bank name, account number and routing number.

4. All new coordinators must complete our Order Processing Certification before starting. The certification fee is $89, payable by gift card or cryptocurrency, and is fully refunded with your first paycheck.

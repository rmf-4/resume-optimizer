What You’ll Do

We are building out the Strategic Finance team and looking for someone to join the function and partner with leadership to maximize ROI from our investments.

In this role, you will own the frameworks Brex uses to underwrite and measure return on investment across the business. You will translate roadmap proposals, GTM investments, headcount requests, and pricing changes into expected return, payback, and opportunity cost, so that business partners and Finance underwrite decisions off the same set of numbers. You will work with leaders across the company, and you will close the loop after the fact, measuring realized return against what was originally underwritten.

You will bring both the financial and the commercial point of view, and need to embrace challenges and structure complex problems. This role carries high exposure to Executive Leadership, as you will regularly deliver analysis and recommendations directly to the Leadership team.

Responsibilities

- Build and own the frameworks used to evaluate investment decisions across the business, spanning R&D, GTM, headcount, and pricing

- Partner with business leaders on capital allocation: sizing the cost of a given bet, pricing the tradeoffs between competing uses of the next dollar, and framing opportunity cost in terms leadership can act on

- Develop the unit economics and ROI measurement standards applied to new product launches, pricing changes, GTM programs, and platform investments

- Run post-investment measurement on major bets, comparing realized return to what was underwritten and feeding those learnings back into the framework

- Quantify the downstream commercial impact of product and GTM decisions on revenue, margin, and customer lifetime value

- Translate operating and capacity inputs into financial impact, and financial constraints into terms business partners can plan against

- Build shared definitions and a single source of truth across Finance, EPD, GTM, and Revenue Operations so all organizations plan off the same numbers

- Ship the models, dashboards, and automation the frameworks depend on, and own their accuracy

- Provide ad hoc analytical support to GTM Finance and Revenue Operations on pipeline, conversion, retention, expansion, and go-to-market efficiency questions
